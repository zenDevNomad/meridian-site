Meridian

A calendar app for macOS that brings your iCloud, Google and Microsoft 365 calendars into one fast, keyboard-friendly window.

What it does

  • Month, week, year and agenda views with Monday-first weeks and calendar-week numbers.
  • Reads and writes events directly through each provider's own API. There is no Meridian server in between.
  • Calendar sets to switch between work and personal calendars in one click.
  • Optional 16-day weather forecast for a location of your choice.

Your data

Meridian runs entirely on your Mac. Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain, calendar data is cached in a local database, and nothing is sent to the developer. Read the privacy policy for details.
